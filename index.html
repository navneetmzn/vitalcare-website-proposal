03 · Why now

Search has changed. Your website now has three audiences.

Websites used to be built for people and for Google's list of links. In 2026 a growing share of health-product research starts with an AI answer, and AI agents can now compare products and complete purchases for people. That is why we plan this website from the point of view of agentic AI.

Audience 1

People

Mostly on phones, many of them older adults and caregivers. They need pages that load quickly, read clearly, work with accessibility tools and feel trustworthy.

We deliver: fast pre-rendered pages, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, reduced-motion support, visible expert authorship.

Audience 2

Google and AI answers

Google says its AI Overviews and AI Mode rely on the same fundamentals as regular search: pages that can be crawled, are fast, and give clear, expert answers. Many AI crawlers do not run JavaScript, so the full content must be in the HTML.

We deliver: server-rendered HTML, complete Schema.org data, question-led content, author and reviewer markup.

Audience 3

AI agents

Assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude can now call a website's tools directly instead of reading it like a person. Google's PageSpeed Insights already scores this as "Agentic Browsing".

We deliver: llms.txt, an MCP server with product and guide tools, WebMCP actions in the browser, an open product API.

Is WordPress good for SEO? Yes, WordPress can rank. Google does not rank sites by the technology behind them, and plugins make basic SEO easy to manage. The problems on vitalcare.mx come from how the site was built up over time, not from WordPress itself. We still recommend the rebuild because ranking in 2026 also depends on speed, structured data and agent access. A purpose-built platform delivers all three by design, while a page builder with 18+ plugins can only be patched toward them.

15 · Fine print

Assumptions and next steps

Assumptions

  • Prices are in USD and exclude taxes and third-party fees (cloud hosting, licences, AI usage, payment, shipping, invoicing, WhatsApp and email providers).
  • The design covers the desktop homepage. We design the other pages and screen sizes in the same style.
  • Base site in Spanish. English is add-on D8.
  • VitalCare provides copy and images for new pages (or approves our drafts), product data and manuals, and a qualified reviewer for health content.
  • Two revision rounds per design deliverable.
  • VitalCare owns all source code, designs and content after final payment. Hosting accounts are in VitalCare's name.
  • We build in support for COFEPRIS advertising requirements and Mexico's data protection law. Legal review of texts remains with VitalCare.

What we need to start

  • Brand assets and the source file of the homepage design
  • Access to the current WordPress site and its hosting
  • Access to Google Search Console and Analytics
  • Read access to DrCare's Shopify catalogue (a custom-app key), arranged with DrCare
  • One decision-maker for approvals

Next steps

  1. Review this proposal and the audit.
  2. A 30-minute call to answer questions and choose the option and modules.
  3. We send the final scope and agreement.
  4. Kick-off within one week of signing.
